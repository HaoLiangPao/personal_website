## Hi! It's Hao here 👋

Welcome to my digital garden, where I put my distilled knowledge. I like reading, researching and trying out new things. There is a lot of wasted time and many failures along the way, but I do capture some valuable thoughts that I believe are worth sharing.

If you accidentally dropped by, please walk around and see if you find anything interesting. You can find my contact information down below, don't be afraid to knock on my "door" :)

## A little bit about myself

I'm an **AI Adoption Lead at BMO** (through UST), based in the Greater Toronto Area 🌱. I architect and ship production agentic RAG systems on AWS, and I lead the cross-functional team behind them.

Before that, I spent 2022–2025 at **IBM** as a software engineer, working on CI/CD for mission-critical compiler products, cloud-native developer tooling, and internal AI systems. Along the way I've also worn the hats of BA, PM, DevOps engineer, and even sales, lab researcher and startup founder. I studied *Environmental Chemistry* and *Computer Science* at the **University of Toronto**, and I'm now adding a formal CS foundation with Georgia Tech's Machine Learning track.

Always keep learning and coding!! 🤘

- 🎿 Ski coach in my spare time
- 🏀 Big fan of basketball and actually any kinds of sports (e-sports too!): 🏃 🏸 🏓 🎱 🚴 🛶 ⚽ 🎮 etc.
